# Cuboards — realistic valuation (South Africa)

_Last reviewed: 2026-10-22_

Internal reference for Andre — not formal appraisal advice.

## Snapshot (Oct 2026)

| Factor | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Product | v1+ : factory/full/client PDFs, **embedded sheet maps in factory/full PDF**, global board/edging catalog, corner blocks + floor screw rules, Gelmar refresh, nesting, VAT, analytics on Vercel |

| Commits | ~75; active development through **22 Oct 2026** |

## Realistic ZAR ranges (private sale / asset, not VC)

| Scenario | Range | Notes |
|----------|-------|--------|
| As-is, no paying users, public repo | **R0 – R50k** | Often **R15k–R40k** for app + deploy handover; many buyers **R0** (can fork GitHub) |
| Private repo + clean deploy/docs | **R30k – R80k** | Exclusivity helps slightly |
| ~15 × ~R200/mo (~R36k/year revenue) | **R50k – R120k** | Tiny B2B SaaS often **1–3× ARR** in SA |
| ~50 × ~R200/mo (~R120k/year, ~R10k/mo) | **R150k – R400k** | Recurring revenue is what acquirers pay for |
| Strategic (supplier/chain + user base) | Variable | Needs **customers/contracts**, not code alone |

**Central estimate today:** **~R20k–R40k** one-off to the right buyer; **R0** to most of the market.

## What increases worth fastest

1. PayFast + Pro tier (R149–R249/mo) and **paid invoices on record**
2. Supabase login + job sync (retention, defensibility)
3. Commercial license or private repo if selling codebase
4. Testimonials + channel (board shops, cabinet Facebook/WhatsApp groups)

## Business vs asset

- **Asset sale today:** low (see table above).
- **Income asset:** business plan target ~50 subs × R200 ≈ **R10k/month** — that cash flow is the real “worth” if Andre operates it.
